# Lazy Loading Strategy for Shopify Stores

Lazy loading improves ecommerce performance by delaying non-essential assets until users need them.

Used correctly, lazy loading can improve:

* Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)
* interaction responsiveness
* perceived speed
* mobile experience

## Best Practices

Recommended lazy-loading strategy:

### Images

Lazy load:

* below-the-fold images
* collection images
* blog visuals
* secondary product media

Do not lazy load:

* hero images
* featured product visuals
* critical above-the-fold content

### JavaScript

Delay:

* analytics tools
* widgets
* reviews
* third-party embeds

### Performance Goal

Fast ecommerce experiences improve:

* conversion rates
* engagement
* SEO performance
* customer trust
